Canada Shift Watcher

Watches the Amazon Canada search page and alerts you with the job location. You make the final selection and confirmation.

Enter one location per line, or separate locations with commas. Choose Anywhere in Canada to disable location filtering.
Automatic preparation opens the first listing, selects the first available shift, clicks Create application, then stops at Submit. If enabled, an alternative schedule offered after the selected slot fills will be accepted automatically. Return to job search after rejecting it and watching resumes automatically.
