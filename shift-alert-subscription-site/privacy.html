Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Canada Shift Watcher collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers visit the website, purchase one-time access, or use the extension.

Information collected

  • Email address used for licensing, activation, or support.
  • Payment confirmation metadata from the checkout provider, such as payment status, plan type, and order references.
  • License token information created after successful payment verification.
  • Basic website analytics if analytics are enabled on the live site.
  • Extension settings needed to apply the customerâ€™s preferences and access level.

How information is used

  • To activate and verify paid access.
  • To provide product support and respond to customer requests.
  • To maintain access controls and prevent unauthorized use.
  • To improve the website and extension experience if analytics are enabled.

Storage and retention

Email addresses, license records, and payment references are retained for the period needed to provide access, support, billing, and fraud prevention, unless a longer period is required by law or accounting obligations.

Security

Secret keys, webhook secrets, and database credentials are kept server-side and are never placed in the browser frontend. Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards should be used to protect stored data.

Sharing

Payment processing is handled by a hosted checkout provider. We do not sell customer data. Data may be shared with service providers only as needed to process payments, send support email, host the website, or operate licensing.

Extension permissions

The extension may request access needed to monitor listings, open official pages, and store user preferences. Those permissions are used to provide the core product functionality described on the site.

Customer rights and contact

Customers may request access, correction, or deletion of their information where applicable. Contact support at SUPPORT_EMAIL.
