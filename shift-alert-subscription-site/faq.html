Frequently Asked Questions

What am I buying?

Customers can download the extension for free, then purchase a Day Activation or a 30-Day Activation to unlock paid access.

Does the extension submit applications for me?

No. The extension may help you notice relevant listings faster and can help with certain intermediate setup screens, but final submission and verification remain manual.

How do I get the extension?

You can download the extension from the website for free. After checkout, your activation license unlocks paid access. When the Chrome Web Store URL is ready, you can connect it in config.js and the site will show the real link.

How do payments work?

Payment buttons on the site point to configurable hosted checkout URLs. Payment verification and license activation must happen on the server after the customer pays.

What is the support email?

The live website should use the value you set in config.js for SUPPORT_EMAIL. See the Contact page for the configured address.

Is Canada Shift Watcher affiliated with any workplace platform?

No. It is an independent product and must not claim affiliation or endorsement unless you have actual authorization.
